# Moo Cam Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

## Summary

Moo Cam does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal data. The photos you take and the settings you choose stay on your device. The app has no internet access, no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

This policy applies to the Moo Cam app for Android (package name kiwi.lorenz.moocam).

## Camera permission

Moo Cam asks for access to your camera. It uses the camera only while the app is open, for three things:

- Showing the live camera preview on screen
- Taking photos when the countdown ends
- Blinking the flashlight, if you turn on the attention flash

The camera image is processed only on your device. It is never sent anywhere. You can withdraw the permission at any time in your phone's Android settings.

## Photos and settings

Photos you take are saved to your phone's gallery, in the folder DCIM/MooCam. From there they are yours: you can view, share or delete them with your gallery app. Moo Cam does not upload them anywhere. If your phone backs up your gallery (for example to Google Photos), that happens under your own settings for that service, not through Moo Cam.

Moo Cam stores your app settings on your device, such as the timer delay and the sound you picked. These settings may be included in your phone's Android backup, if you have that turned on. They contain no personal information.

## No data collection or sharing

Moo Cam does not have permission to access the internet, so it cannot send data off your device. In particular, Moo Cam:

- Collects no personal data, device identifiers or location
- Has no user accounts or sign-in
- Shows no ads and contains no advertising SDKs
- Uses no analytics or crash-reporting services
- Shares no data with third parties

## Children

Moo Cam is made for parents and caregivers who photograph young children. It is meant to be used by adults, not by the children themselves. Photos of children taken with the app stay on the adult's device, like every other photo, and Moo Cam collects no information about them.

## Changes and contact
